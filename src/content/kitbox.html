✦ PRODUCT OVERVIEW

Kitbox is a mobile e-commerce app for a home-goods brand designed to eliminate mobile drop-offs. It transforms a cluttered discovery flow into a frictionless, end-to-end shopping journey within the brand's ecosystem.

✦ DESIGN SOLUTIONS (SOLO UX/UI)
  • 3-Step Optimized Checkout: Redesigned the entire purchase funnel into a high-conversion, 3-step checkout to minimize drop-offs.
  • Scalable UI System: Created a design system with precise tokens and components for direct iOS development.
  • Core Architecture: Designed all high-impact screens from smart category filtering to interactive order tracking.
✦ BUSINESS & UX IMPACT
  • Zero Friction: Drastically reduced navigation complexity from discovery to final order confirmation.
  • Dev-Ready Delivery: Partnered 1:1 with an iOS developer, delivering buildable, production-ready Figma specs.
  • Future-Proof: Left the brand with a reusable UI kit to launch future features without design debt.
