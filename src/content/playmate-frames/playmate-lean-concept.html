07Concept

The app in three steps.

1Setting up a profile

You pick your sports, set your skill level and say how you like to play.

Your profile is your reputation.

2Finding or creating a game

You browse open game rooms near you by sport, level, time and cost. You can join in one tap or start your own.

Scheduling, cost split and waitlist are handled for you.

3Playing and rating

After the game, you rate your teammates on reliability, passion, respect and communication.

The more reliably you show up, the easier it is to get into games.

Find a matchYou filter games by sport, skill and time.
Game roomYou see the price per person, the level and who’s playing before you join.
After the gameYou say whether each teammate showed up and rate them on four things.
Your profileSkill, reliability and reviews add up to a reputation.
