CONCEPT

The app in four steps.

1

Make a category.

Give it a name, an icon and a colour. Drag the chips to reorder them.

2

Save it.

Tap the plus button and add a value to a category.

3

Find it.

Spot it by colour, filter by category, search or sort.

4

Copy it.

Tap the card to copy the value. Values stay hidden until you tap the eye.

One tap away.
