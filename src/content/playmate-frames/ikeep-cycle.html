CONCEPT

The app in four steps.

1

Making a category

Each category gets a name, an icon and a colour. You drag the chips to reorder them.

2

Saving a value

You tap the plus button and add a value to a category.

3

Finding a value

You filter by category, search or sort.

4

Copying a value

Tapping the card copies the value. Values stay hidden until you tap the eye.

One tap away.
