10Build with the kit

Rules for a new Chengel screen.

1Purple acts, blue writes.

Buttons and progress are purple. Only the letters a player types are blue; nothing else is ever blue.

2One typeface

JetBrains Mono for everything. Yagmur Hand only for letters inside the crossword.

3Thin lines

1px for the grid and cards, 1.5px for buttons and icons, 2px for whatever is active.

4Every status has an icon and words.

Colour is never the only cue.

5Every text pair meets 4.5:1

In light and dark.

6Copy is short and in sentence case.

Exclamation marks only on success. Languages get a two-letter code, never a flag.
