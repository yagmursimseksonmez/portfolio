Playing sport regularly is easy to want and hard to organise.

QUESTION

How can I make finding and organising a game easier, and build enough trust for people to play with strangers?

HYPOTHESIS

People already want to play. What holds them back is the effort of finding the right person, and not trusting that person or the system.

CONFIRMED

All 4 players I interviewed already had a sport routine. All of them struggled with coordination, skill matching or trusting someone new.
