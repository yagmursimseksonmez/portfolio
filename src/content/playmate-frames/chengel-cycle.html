CONCEPT

Four steps that repeat with every puzzle.

Follow the path.

The path shows where you are. When you tap the start node, the puzzle opens.

Solve the puzzle.

The clues are in your language and the answers in the one you learn. A letter hint costs 10 coins and a word hint 30.

Practice it.

Solved words go into three folders: Knew it, Knew with letters, Mostly revealed. Flash cards bring them back.

Grow a garden.

Each level from A1 to C1 has a garden with eight blooms. The words you learn plant them.
