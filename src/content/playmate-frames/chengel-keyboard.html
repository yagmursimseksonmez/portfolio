Iterations · keyboard and dark mode

Two things I tuned: the keyboard was louder than the puzzle, and the dark colours tired the eyes.

Problem

Keys had a 1px outline, and it made the letters harder to read. In dark mode the light areas added up, and the keyboard stood out more than the puzzle.

Options

Four keyboards, each in light and dark: soft keys, tonal keys, lilac keys, letters only.

What I chose

Tonal keys. No outline, each key a hair lighter than the panel.

Why

I checked the contrast of each one and picked the smallest step between key and panel that still passes. The lighter the key, the lighter the letter has to be to pass, and at night that glares. In light mode it works the same way, the other way round.

The risk

Muted keys could be harder to read. Checked: letters stay at 8.4:1 in light and 6.8:1 in dark.

Four options. I chose number 2, tonal keys.
