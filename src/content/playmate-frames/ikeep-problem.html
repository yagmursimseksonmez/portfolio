THE BRIEF

I was asked to rebrand iKeep, an iOS vault for the values people copy again and again.

WHAT I FOUND

Every item looked the same. You had to read each row to find the one to copy. The Add button sat at the top right, out of thumb reach.

WHAT I DID

In the same week I also redesigned how it works, with cards, thumb-first actions and ways to organise items.
