CONCEPT

The app in three steps.

1

Set up a profile.

Pick your sports, set your skill level and say how you like to play.

Your profile is your reputation.
2

Find or create a game.

Browse open game rooms near you by sport, level, time and cost. Join in one tap, or start your own.

Scheduling, cost split and waitlist are handled for you.
3

Play, rate, repeat.

After the game, rate your teammates on reliability, passion, respect and communication.

The more reliably you show up, the easier it is to get into games.
