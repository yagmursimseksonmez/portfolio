Chengel · iOS · Freelance · Konkret

Learn words by solving crosswords.

Chengel is an iOS app for learning vocabulary. The clue is in your language and the answer is in the one you are learning. You write it in my own handwriting, like filling in a newspaper crossword.

SITUATION

Word apps feel like homework

Lists and flash cards work, but they feel like studying. I wanted learning words to feel like a game you already know.

TASK

I designed the app, the design system, the icons and the illustrations. One iOS developer built it.

ACTION

A crossword in my handwriting

I tried four keyboards in light and dark. I chose tonal keys over outlined ones, so the puzzle stays louder than the keys.

RESULT

Every text pair passes 4.5:1 contrast. It has not been tested with learners yet.
