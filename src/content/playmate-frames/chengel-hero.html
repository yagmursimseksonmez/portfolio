Chengel · iOS · submitted to the App Store

Learn words by solving crosswords.

Chengel is an iOS app for learning vocabulary. The clue is in your language, the answer is in the one you are learning, and you write it in my own handwriting, like filling in a newspaper crossword.

ROLE

Designer, with one iOS developer

I designed the app, the design system, the icons and the illustrations. One iOS developer built it.

PROBLEM

Word apps feel like homework

Lists and flash cards work, but they feel like studying. I wanted it to feel like a game you already know.

SOLUTION

A crossword with a garden

Solve a puzzle, keep the words you learned, and plant a flower for every puzzle.

IMPACT

Built in 3 weeks, now in review

Designed and built Oct 1–20, 2026. It is waiting for Apple’s review.
