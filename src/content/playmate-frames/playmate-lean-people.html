03Interviews

I interviewed 4 people on Zoom.

Each of them already had a sport routine, and each talk took 30 minutes.

Method

Semi-structured interviews and desk research.

Found

I found them at a few gyms, in my language course and around me.

Prepared

I wrote 16 open questions and cut them down to fit 30 minutes.

Ran

I asked for consent first. On Zoom I typed notes live and recorded the audio to fill the gaps.
