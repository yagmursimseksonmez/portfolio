WHAT I DID

I designed Chengel, an iOS crossword for learning words: the app, the design system, the icons and the illustrations.

HOW I DID IT

I took the typography of my own handwriting for the puzzle letters, drew the icons by hand, polished them with AI, and built the design in Claude Design.

WHY I DID IT

I wanted learning words to feel like the newspaper crosswords we used to fill in by hand.

WHO WAS INVOLVED

Me for the design, and one iOS developer who built it. It is now in App Store review.
