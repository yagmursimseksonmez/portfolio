WHAT I DID

I designed Chengel, an iOS crossword for learning words. I also made its design system, icons and illustrations.

HOW I DID IT

I made a typeface from my own handwriting for the puzzle letters. I drew the icons by hand and polished them with AI.

WHY I DID IT

I wanted learning words to feel like the newspaper crosswords we used to fill in by hand.

WHO WAS INVOLVED

I did the design, and one iOS developer built it.
