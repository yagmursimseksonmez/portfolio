CONCEPT

The daily loop in five steps.

1

Check your HP.

Steps, sleep, water, mood and more become one HP score.

2

Get one suggestion.

Master Ronnie suggests one small, low-pressure thing: a short walk, one calm glass.

A mentor, not a monitor.
3

Act on it.

Breathe, reflect, check in your mood or log water in one tap.

4

Read your day.

Your day becomes a game-style combat log, with trend cards and tiny quests.

5

Level up.

Your character grows: average stats, the HP trend and achievements.
