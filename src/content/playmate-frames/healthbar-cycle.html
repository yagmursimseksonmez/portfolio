CONCEPT

The daily loop in five steps.

1

You check your HP.

Steps, sleep, water, mood and more become one HP score.

2

You get one suggestion.

Master Ronnie suggests one small, low-pressure thing, like a short walk.

A mentor, not a monitor.
3

You act on it.

Breathing, reflecting and logging your mood or water each take one tap.

4

You read your day.

Your day becomes a game-style combat log, with trend cards and tiny quests.

5

Your character levels up.

Over time you see your average stats and earn achievements.
