Idea

I wanted learning words to feel like an old newspaper crossword.

QUESTION

How can learning vocabulary be fun, like we used to do in old times? Solving a crossword with a pencil.

IDEA

A crossword where the clue is in your language and the answer is the word you are learning.

REFERENCE

We used to solve newspaper crosswords by hand. The app brings that back, down to the handwriting. But this time it helps you learn a language.
