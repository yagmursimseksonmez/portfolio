Kitbox · iOS · freelance

A furniture shop, from website to iPhone.

Kitbox sells home and office furniture and only had a website. As their only designer, I designed their first iOS app end to end, working with the client and the iOS developer who built it.

ROLE

The only designer, freelance

UX/UI and the design system, working with the client and an iOS developer.

PROBLEM

A website, but no app

Customers could only shop on the website. The brand wanted its own shopping app.

SOLUTION

A short way to checkout

Onboarding, browsing and product pages, with checkout kept to 3 steps.

IMPACT

Their first app, on the App Store

The developer built it from my component kit, which the brand can reuse for new features.
