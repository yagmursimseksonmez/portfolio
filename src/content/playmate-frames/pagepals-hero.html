PAGEPALS · case study · social reading web app

Find your next book through your friends.

I redesigned a three-year-old survey project. The shelf is the home, and friends’ reading drives discovery.

ROLE

Research, UX, UI, design system

I re-read the research and built every screen and the design system.

PROBLEM

Friends find you books. Apps don’t help.

73.3% discover books through friends. Only 13.3% like the social side of today’s apps.

SOLUTION

The shelf is the home

Four reading states, friends’ activity first, and a reason on every recommendation.
