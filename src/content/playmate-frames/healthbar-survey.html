SURVEY

I surveyed 13 people, ages 21–45, about the health apps they use.

8 of 13

have muted health-app notifications that felt guilt-inducing or repetitive

7 of 13

find charts and trend graphs hard to act on without context

9 of 13

would check a health app more often if it felt encouraging, not evaluative

7 of 13

want one clear suggestion a day, not a dashboard of metrics

6 of 13

say a widget or watch glance matters more than opening the app

“I see the line drop, I don’t know why.”

one of the people I surveyed
