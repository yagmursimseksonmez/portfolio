PROBLEM

I surveyed 15 readers, ages 18–40, who use book platforms. These results set the problems I designed for.

73.3%

discover new books through friends and family

66.7%

name lack of personalization as their top frustration

60%

want to see what the people they follow are reading

53.3%

want to track reading status and share it

53.3%

are interested in virtual clubs and groups

40%

complain of complex UI and limited engagement

33.3%

are motivated by rewards or challenges, the lowest incentive

13.3%

like the social side of the platforms they use today

92.9%

find recommendations at least moderately effective (n = 14)
