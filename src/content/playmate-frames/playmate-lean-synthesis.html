The six themes

Heard, not builtTurned into a feature

1What motivates them to play
  • Meeting friends, well-being
  • Learning a new technique
  • Fun, sweat, a mood boost
  • Progress and socialising
  • A plan, so skipping is not an option
2Who they want to play with
  • Strangers at my level×3
  • Friends×1
3Time and place preferences
  • WhatsApp group + poll×3
  • Turn up at the gym and find someone×1
4Trust factors
  • “Mindset matters more than looks. Ego is a red flag.”
  • Wants people her age who talk with respect
  • Reviews and ratings, like Airbnb
  • “I don’t care if they’re trustworthy”
5Pain points
  • Finding a time that suits everyone
  • Finding a place for 12 people
  • Bringing people together
  • Finding someone of similar size and mindset
6Features they wished the app had
  • A favourite playmates list
  • A set time every week
  • Rate people on reliability, passion, respect…
  • Skill level on the profile
  • “Why do you do sports?” on the profile
  • “At what time I’ll arrive”
  • Reminders to play

What it meansWhat I designed

The plan lives in a chat nobody can follow.

→

Game roomsTime, place, player limits and cost split in one room.

Level matters more than knowing someone.

→

Skill levelsOn every profile and game, and as a filter.

Trust has to be visible before the first game.

→

Peer ratingsReliability, passion, respect and communication, after every game.

A no-show costs the people who did show up.

→

Waitlist and no-show penaltyThe next person fills the spot. A no-show lowers reliability.

A good match should be easy to repeat.

→

Friends and repeat gamesFavourite playmates are one tap away, and you can repeat a game every week.
