02Concept

The app in four steps.

1Making a category

Each category gets a name, an icon and a colour. You drag the chips to reorder them.

2Saving a value

You tap the plus button and add a value to a category.

3Finding a value

You filter by category, search or sort.

4Copying a value

Tapping the card copies the value. Values stay hidden until you tap the eye.

One tap away.

Your cardsEvery item is its own card, with its category next to the title. You tap a card to copy its value.
New itemYou add an item from the button in thumb reach, with a category, title and value.
SearchSearch looks through everything you saved.
Dark modeEvery screen comes in light and dark mode.
