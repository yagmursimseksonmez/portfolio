Decision · category symbolsRunning since 2 November

Will a symbol help people find the card to copy?

Problem

In All items, each card shows its category only as small text. People read card by card to find the one to copy.

Options

A is the list as it is today. B adds the category’s symbol to the coloured badge on each card. The widgets and the Watch already use this symbol.

Why B might win

Colour and shape are seen before text.

How I’ll decide

The main measure is how many people copy an item within 5 seconds of opening the list. B only wins if people don’t copy the wrong card more often. The test runs for at least two weeks.

What it costs

iKeep is small, so each group is small. That’s enough to see a direction, but it’s not proof. I’ll also watch 3–5 people use it in person.
