Will a symbol help people find the card to copy?

Problem

In All items, every card shows its category only as small text, so people read card by card to find the one they want to copy.

Options

A: the list as it is today. B: the same card with the category’s symbol on its coloured badge, the one the widgets and the Watch already use.

Why B might win

Colour and shape are seen before text.

How I’ll decide

The main measure is how many people copy an item within 5 seconds of opening the list. B only wins if people don’t copy the wrong card more often. It runs for at least two weeks.

What it costs

iKeep is small, so each group will be small. That’s enough to see a direction, but it’s not proof. I’ll also watch 3–5 people use it in person.
