01The problem

Health apps show you numbers, but they don’t suggest a next step.

I surveyed 13 people, ages 21–45, about the health apps they use.

8 of 13have muted health-app notifications that felt guilt-inducing or repetitive
7 of 13want one clear suggestion a day
9 of 13would check a health app more often if it felt encouraging

“I see the line drop, I don’t know why.”One of the people I surveyed
