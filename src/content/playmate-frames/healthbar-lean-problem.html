01The problem

Health apps show you numbers, but they don’t suggest a next step.

Question

How can Apple Health data feel calm and encouraging, and tell you what to do next?

Hypothesis

If their data reads like a story with a mentor, people will understand their day at a glance and find one thing to do.

What the survey showed

8 of the 13 people I surveyed had muted health-app notifications that felt guilt-inducing or repetitive.
