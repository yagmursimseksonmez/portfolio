WHAT I DID

I designed Kitbox’s first iOS shopping app, end to end: onboarding, browsing, product pages and a 3-step checkout.

HOW I DID IT

I worked with the client and the iOS developer, and built a component kit, colours and type the developer could build from directly.

WHY I DID IT

Kitbox only sold through its website. The brand wanted customers to shop from their phone.

WHO WAS INVOLVED

Me as the only designer, the client and one iOS developer.
