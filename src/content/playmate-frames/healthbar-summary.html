WHAT I DID

I designed and shipped HealthBar with an iOS developer. It is an RPG-style Apple Health companion for iOS, Apple Watch and widgets.

HOW I DID IT

I surveyed 13 people and built the screens in Stitch with my own styleset. Then I tested the first dashboard and the redesign with 9 people each, and released it.

WHY I DID IT

Most health apps give you numbers and notifications that feel guilt-inducing or repetitive. I wanted one that kindly tells you what to do next.

WHO WAS INVOLVED

I did the research, UX/UI and usability tests. An iOS developer built the app, and we made the product decisions together. The survey had 13 participants, and each version had 9 testers.
