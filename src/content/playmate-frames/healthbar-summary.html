WHAT I DID

I designed and shipped HealthBar with an iOS developer: an RPG-style Apple Health companion for iOS, Apple Watch and widgets.

HOW I DID IT

I surveyed 13 people, built the screens in Stitch with my own styleset, tested the first dashboard and the redesign with 9 people each, and released it.

WHY I DID IT

Most health apps give you numbers, and notifications that feel guilt-inducing or repetitive. I wanted one that tells you, kindly, what to do next.

WHO WAS INVOLVED

Me for the research, UX/UI and usability tests, and an iOS developer who built it; we made the product decisions together. 13 survey participants and 9 testers per version.
