06Personas and journey map

There are two kinds of player.

I designed for Summer first and checked every flow against Leo.

Summer

Primary persona · The social player

Age: 28 · Interior designer · Berlin, Germany

“The sport isn’t the hard part. Meeting strangers is.”

She represents the “Community” and “Loneliness” side of the interviews.

Bio

She moved to a new city six months ago. Finding reliable, like-minded partners feels exhausting and uncertain.

Personality

Pain points

  • Can’t tell if a stranger is respectful or there to dominate
  • Tired of looking for new people every time

Needs

  • Reviews and commitment ratings
  • Profiles that say why someone plays
  • A favourite playmates list

Primary motivation

To find a reliable “tribe” and turn exercise into a long-term social activity.

Leo

Secondary persona · The efficiency seeker

Age: 35 · Psychologist · Berlin, Germany

“If the game feels unreliable, I stay home.”

He represents the “Logistics” and “Reliability” side of the interviews.

Bio

He is a busy professional, and sport lifts his mood. Planning a game tires him out.

Personality

IntrovertExtrovert
AnalyticalCreative
BusyTime rich
MessyOrganized
IndependentTeam player
PassiveActive
SafeRisky

Pain points

  • Endless group chat to agree on a time
  • Last-minute cancellations and skill gaps

Needs

  • Scheduling from poll results
  • Reliability scores and skill filters
  • An “I’ll arrive at…” status

Primary motivation

Efficiency, consistency, and a “no-drama” experience.
