Usability test

Method

Moderated, think-aloud. 5 readers aged 18–40, about 30 minutes each.

Measures

Task completion, time, errors, and a 1–5 ease rating per task.

Success criteria

At least 4 of 5 complete each task unaided.

Results

To be added after the sessions.

TASKS
  1. 1Sign in and find what you are currently reading.
  2. 2Find a book your friends are reading and add it to “Want to read”.
  3. 3Find a group and join it.
  4. 4Start a challenge against a friend.
  5. 5Open a book and leave a rating.
