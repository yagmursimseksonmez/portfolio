Decision · the home screen

The developer wanted more data on the screen. I wanted one thing to lead.

Situation

In the first usability test, 4 of 9 testers read their HP right at first look. A correct read took 13 seconds on average.

Task

Make the home screen readable at a glance. The developer and I had different ideas about how.

Action

The developer wanted to show more of the data: steps, sleep and water, each on its own. I wanted one thing to lead, and pointed to the survey: 7 of 13 people wanted one clear suggestion a day, not a dashboard of metrics. We kept the data, under the HP: the HP bar sits big at the top, the values one by one below on the left, Master Ronnie’s tip on the right. HP got one dominant colour, and body copy dropped a size.

Result

In the second test, 8 of 9 read their HP right at first look, and a correct read took 4 seconds instead of 13.
