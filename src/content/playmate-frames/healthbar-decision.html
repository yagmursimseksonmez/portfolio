Decision · the home screen

Two ideas for the home screen

The developer wanted more data on the screen, and I wanted one thing to lead.

Situation

In the first usability test, 4 of 9 testers read their HP right at first look. A correct read took 13 seconds on average.

Task

The home screen had to be readable at a glance. The developer and I had different ideas about how.

Action

The developer wanted to show steps, sleep and water, each on its own. I wanted one thing to lead and pointed to the survey, where 7 of 13 people wanted one clear suggestion a day. We kept the data but placed it under the HP bar. HP got one dominant colour, and body copy dropped a size.

Result

In the second test, 8 of 9 read their HP right at first look, and a correct read took 4 seconds instead of 13.
