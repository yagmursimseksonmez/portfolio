HealthBar · iOS, Apple Watch · live on the App Store

Your health, turned into a health bar.

It started as an MVP with one idea: show Apple Health data the way an RPG shows a character’s health. The pixel-art look comes from that.

1
THE PROBLEM

Health apps hand you numbers, but not a next step.

8 of the 13 people I surveyed had muted health-app notifications that felt guilt-inducing or repetitive.

2
WHAT I DID

One score and one suggestion.

All the Health data becomes one HP bar, and Master Ronnie suggests one small thing to do next.

3
THE EVIDENCE

4 → 8 of 9testers read their HP right at first look.

I tested the first dashboard and the redesign with 9 people each.

Not measured yet: whether people check in more often once the app is live.

Me: research, UX/UI and usability tests. The iOS developer built it, and we made the product decisions together. Feb 2025 – now.
