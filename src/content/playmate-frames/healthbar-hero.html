HealthBar · iOS, Apple Watch · Freelance · Konkret

Your health, turned into a health bar.

It started as an MVP that shows Apple Health data the way an RPG shows a character’s health. The pixel-art look comes from that idea.

SITUATION

Health data, but no next step

8 of the 13 people I surveyed had muted health-app notifications that felt guilt-inducing or repetitive. 9 of 13 said they’d check a health app more often if it felt encouraging.

TASK

I did the research, UX/UI and usability tests. The iOS developer built it, and we made the product decisions together.

ACTION

The HP bar comes first

The developer wanted more data on the home screen. I pointed to the survey, where 7 of 13 wanted one clear suggestion a day. Now the HP bar sits at the top, and Master Ronnie suggests one small thing to do next.

RESULT

HP read in 4 seconds, down from 13

After the redesign, 8 of 9 testers read their HP right at first look, up from 4 of 9. It has been live on the App Store since July, and 105 people have downloaded it.

Not measured yet: whether people check in more often.
