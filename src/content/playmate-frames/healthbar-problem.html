Health apps hand you numbers, but not a next step.

QUESTION

How can Apple Health data feel calm and encouraging, and tell you what to do next?

HYPOTHESIS

People will check in more often, and act on what they see, if their data is framed as a story with a mentor instead of a spreadsheet with a verdict.

CONFIRMED

8 of the 13 people I surveyed had muted a health app because its notifications made them feel guilty.
