iKeep · iOS · Freelance · Konkret · live on the App Store

Store the things to remember.

You put them in categories, so finding and copying them is fast.

iKeep is a vault for the values you type again and again, like Wi-Fi passwords and door codes. I was asked to rebrand it, and in one week I redesigned how it works too.

SITUATION

Every item looked the same

Items were identical rows, so you had to read each one to find what to copy. The Add button also sat out of thumb reach.

TASK

Designed and coded in one week

The brief was a rebrand. I took it from design to code. The iOS developer who first built iKeep handles releases and builds.

ACTION

Rows became colour-coded cards

A new look alone would still make people read every row. I turned the rows into colour-coded cards, moved Add into thumb reach and added categories you can reorder. Then I coded it myself with Claude.

RESULT

Live within the week

After launch, the App Store page got 52% more views in 28 days (300 vs 198), but downloads stayed flat (123 vs 120).
