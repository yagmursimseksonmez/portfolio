iKeep · iOS · live on the App Store

Store the things to remember.

Categorize. Find. Copy and paste them. Super fast.

A vault for the values you type again and again, like Wi-Fi passwords and door codes. I was asked to rebrand it, and in one week I redesigned how it works too.

ROLE

Rebrand and redesign

The brief was a new look. I took the app end to end, and worked with the iOS developer who built it.

PROBLEM

Every item looked the same

You had to read each row to find the one to copy, and Add sat out of thumb reach.

SOLUTION

Cards you can spot at a glance

Colour-coded cards, Add in thumb reach, categories you can reorder, sorting and dark mode.

IMPACT

Find it by colour, not by reading

The new version shipped within the week and is live on the App Store.
