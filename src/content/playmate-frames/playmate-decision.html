Decision · no-shows

What happens when someone doesn’t show up?

Problem

Testers asked what happens when someone drops out.

Options

A warning message, or a lost reliability star.

What I chose

A confirmed player who doesn’t come loses a reliability star, and it shows on their profile.

Why

A warning stays private. A star is something others see before they join, so they can decide who to trust. It’s light but visible: no money, no ban, nothing that pushes players out of the app.

What it costs

A star doesn’t stop people the way a fee does. Someone who doesn’t care about stars may still not show up.
