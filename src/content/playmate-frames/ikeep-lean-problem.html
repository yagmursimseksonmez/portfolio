01The problem

It started as a new look, but the bigger problem was finding the right value.

The rebrand

In 2026 I rebranded iKeep, the iOS vault for the values people copy again and again. I started working on its design in 2025.

What I found

Every item looked the same. You had to read each row to find the one to copy. The Add button sat at the top right, out of thumb reach.

What I did

In the same week I also redesigned how it works, with cards, thumb-first actions and ways to organise items.
