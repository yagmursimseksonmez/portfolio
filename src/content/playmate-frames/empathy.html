EMPATHY MAP

What a player says, does, feels and thinks.

SAYS

Wants to play, but doesn’t know if the court is free or who will be there.

DOES

Searches Maps, Facebook and Reddit. Deletes apps that are inactive or too expensive.

FEELS

Nervous about being judged, isolated in a new city, hopeful about finding a “tribe”.

THINKS

What if I join and can’t make it? I want to know the people will actually show up.

PAIN

Flaky partners, mismatched skill levels, social anxiety, no court information.

GAIN

A consistent playmate group, a place in a new city, better health, a reliability rating that builds trust.
