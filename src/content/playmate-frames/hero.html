PlayMate · solo project · community sports app

Find people to play sport with.

PlayMate is a mobile app that matches people for sport. Instead of a group chat, you join a game room that shows the time, place, cost and who’s playing. Profiles show each player’s skill level, peer ratings and no-show penalties, so you can trust them before you meet.

SITUATION

Organising a game is chaos

All 4 players I interviewed had a routine, and all struggled with coordination, skill matching or trusting someone new.

TASK

I ran the interviews and usability tests and designed every screen.

ACTION

Game rooms and player reputations

When testers asked about no-shows, I chose a reliability star that others can see instead of a private warning.

RESULT

4 test rounds, 4 iterations

After each round I fixed what testers stumbled on. I added cost splitting, waitlists and a rating flow, and I made the colours calmer. There are no live users yet.
