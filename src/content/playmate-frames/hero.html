PlayMate · ReDI School bootcamp project · community sports app

Wanna do sports but don't have a team/partner? I have a solution!

This is a mobile app that matches people for sport. Instead of a group chat, you join a game room: you see the time, the place, the cost, who's playing. And the team members? You can check their profile to see skill levels, peer ratings and a no-show penalty, and build trust beforehand.

ROLE

Solo, from interviews to prototype

I ran the interviews and the usability tests, and designed every screen.

PROBLEM

Organising a game, why pure chaos?

All 4 players I interviewed had a routine, and all struggled with coordination, skill matching or trusting someone new.

SOLUTION

Game rooms with reputations

Schedule, split the cost and play! No place left? Join the waitlist.

IMPACT

Tested at every step, 4 iterations

With the testers' feedback, I added cost splitting, waitlists and a rating flow. Made the visual style calmer.
