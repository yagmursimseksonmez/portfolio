05Iterations · keyboard and dark mode

I tuned the keyboard and the dark colours.

Problem

Each key had a 1px outline that made the letters harder to read. In dark mode, the keyboard stood out more than the puzzle.

Options

I tried four keyboards, each in light and dark: soft keys, tonal keys, lilac keys and letters only.

What I chose

Tonal keys. They have no outline, and each key is a hair lighter than the panel.

Why

I checked the contrast of each one and picked the smallest step between key and panel that still passes. A lighter key needs a lighter letter to pass, and at night that glares.

Four options. I chose number 2, tonal keys.
The risk

Muted keys could be harder to read. I checked, and the letters stay at 8.4:1 in light and 6.8:1 in dark.
