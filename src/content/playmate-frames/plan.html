1
PLANNING

I planned the research before I talked to anyone.

1
PROJECT BACKGROUND

PlayMate aims to fight social isolation through sport. I started with four hypotheses to test.

2
RESEARCH GOALS

I wanted to learn what frustrates people when they organise sport through WhatsApp or Facebook, and which profile details make meeting a stranger feel safe.

3
RESEARCH METHODS

Semi-structured interviews and desk research.

4
RESEARCH QUESTIONS

The interviews had to answer seven questions, such as what happens when a partner cancels.

5
PARTICIPANT RECRUITMENT

I looked for people with a sport routine. I planned six sessions of 30 minutes each, on Zoom or in person, and ran them myself.

6
CONSTRAINTS

An app could leave out older athletes who are less comfortable with digital tools. Skill levels are self-reported, and new users have no reliability rating yet.

7
DATA ANALYSIS

I planned to sort the data in Excel and FigJam. Recordings and notes stay on my own devices.

8
DELIVERABLES AND TIMELINE

I planned four phases: discovery, UX design, UI design and a prototype. Each one had its own deliverables, from personas to final screens.

★
RESEARCH JAM

Every interview covered the same topics, like sport routine, community and trust. The questions changed with each person.
