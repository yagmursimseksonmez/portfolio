PLAN

Before talking to anyone, I wanted to plan the research and decide how to move forward.

1
PROJECT BACKGROUND

PlayMate is a community app that connects people through sport to fight social isolation. Four hypotheses to test, and the project is in the exploration phase.

2
RESEARCH GOALS

Understand what frustrates people when they organise sport through WhatsApp or Facebook. Find which profile information makes meeting a stranger feel safe, and which features keep people playing regularly.

3
RESEARCH METHODS

Semi-structured interviews and desk research.

4
RESEARCH QUESTIONS

Seven questions the interviews had to answer, such as how people find sports partners today, what makes them trust a stranger, and what happens when a partner cancels.

5
PARTICIPANT RECRUITMENT

People who already have a sport routine, 30 minutes each, on Zoom or in person. I run the sessions myself; six were planned.

12345678★
6
CONSTRAINTS

An app could leave out older athletes who are less comfortable with digital tools. Skill levels are self-reported and hard to verify, and new users start with no reliability rating.

7
DATA ANALYSIS

Zoom for the interviews, a phone to record, paper for notes. Data is organised in Excel and clustered in FigJam, and recordings and notes stay on my own devices.

8
DELIVERABLES AND TIMELINE

Four phases: discovery (report, personas, journey map), UX design (wireframes, information architecture), UI design (style guide, design system, final screens) and a prototype.

★
RESEARCH JAM

The anchor topics stay fixed (daily sport routine, community, social circle, motivation, trust). The questions change with each person.
