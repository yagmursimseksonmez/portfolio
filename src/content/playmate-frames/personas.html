Bio

Moved to a new city six months ago. She sees sports as a gateway to community and long-term friendship, but finding reliable, like-minded partners feels exhausting and uncertain. The real barrier isn’t the activity, it’s the anxiety of the unknown.

Primary personaThe social player
“The sport isn’t the hard part. Meeting strangers is.”

Stands for the players who want a circle, not just a workout.

PAIN POINTS
  • Can’t tell if a stranger is respectful or there to dominate
  • Tired of looking for new people every time
NEEDS
  • Reviews and commitment ratings
  • Profiles that say why someone plays
  • A favourite playmates list
