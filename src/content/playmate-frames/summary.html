WHAT I DID

I designed PlayMate end to end: a community app that matches people for sport through game rooms instead of group chats.

HOW I DID IT

I planned the research, interviewed 4 people, sorted the notes into themes and turned them into features. Then I tested the wireframes and each hi-fi version.

WHY I DID IT

You want to do sport regularly. Deciding is easy, but the hard part is finding a partner, trusting them and finally organising the game. I wanted to solve that problem.

WHO WAS INVOLVED

It is a bootcamp project. 10 mentors, 5 classmates, 4 interviewees, 4 testers and me.
