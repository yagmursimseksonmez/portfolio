WHAT I DID

I designed PlayMate end to end. It is a community app that matches people for sport through game rooms instead of group chats.

HOW I DID IT

I planned the research and interviewed 4 people. I sorted the notes into themes and turned them into features. Then I tested the wireframes and each hi-fi version.

WHY I DID IT

Deciding to do sport regularly is easy. The hard part is finding a partner, trusting them and organising the game. I wanted to solve that problem.

WHO WAS INVOLVED

It was a project with 4 interviewees, 4 testers and me.
