Usability testing

I tested the first dashboard and the redesign.

Each version had 9 different testers, all friends and acquaintances.

Their task was to open the app and log how many glasses of water they drank.

In the redesign, HP got one dominant colour on a quieter background, and body copy dropped a full size below the headers. I let one thing lead instead of adding more explanation.
