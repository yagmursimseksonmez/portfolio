WHAT I DID

I rebranded and redesigned iKeep, an iOS vault for the values people copy again and again.

HOW I DID IT

I mapped the information architecture, turned list rows into colour-coded cards, moved Add into thumb reach and designed light and dark mode.

WHY I DID IT

The brief was a new look, but the app was hard to use: every item looked the same. A new look alone wouldn’t fix that.

WHO WAS INVOLVED

Me as the designer and an iOS developer, in one week.
