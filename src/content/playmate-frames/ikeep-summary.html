WHAT I DID

I rebranded and redesigned iKeep, an iOS vault for the values people copy again and again.

HOW I DID IT

I mapped the information architecture and turned the list rows into colour-coded cards. I moved Add into thumb reach and designed light and dark mode. Then I coded it myself with Claude.

WHY I DID IT

The brief was a new look, but the app was hard to use because every item looked the same. A new look alone wouldn’t fix that.

WHO WAS INVOLVED

I designed the new iKeep and coded it with Claude in one week. The iOS developer who first built iKeep handles releases and builds.
