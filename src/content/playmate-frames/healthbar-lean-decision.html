03Decision · the home screen

The HP bar comes first.

Problem

In the first test, 4 of 9 read their HP correctly at first glance.

Options

The developer wanted to show steps, sleep and water, each on its own. I wanted one thing to lead.

What we chose

We kept the data but placed it under the HP bar. HP got one dominant colour, and body copy dropped a size.

Why

In the survey, 7 of 13 people wanted one clear suggestion a day.
