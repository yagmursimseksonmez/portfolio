03Concept

You check your HP and get one tip for the day.

1You check your HP.

Steps, sleep, water, mood and more become one HP score.

2You get one suggestion.

Master Ronnie suggests one small, low-pressure thing, like a short walk.

A mentor, not a monitor.

3You act on it.

Breathing, reflecting and logging your mood or water each take one tap.

4You read your day.

Your day becomes a game-style combat log, with trend cards and tiny quests.

5Your character levels up.

Over time you see your average stats and earn achievements.

Your HPThe HP bar, your day in numbers and Master Ronnie’s tip.
Quick actionsYou can breathe, reflect and log your mood or water.
Your characterYour character’s average stats.
AchievementsAchievements, daily goals and difficulty.
