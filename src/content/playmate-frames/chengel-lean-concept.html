03Concept

Four steps that repeat with every puzzle.

1 · Follow the pathThe path shows where you are. Every stop is a puzzle. When you tap the start node, the puzzle opens. When you finish one, a flower grows.
2 · Solve the puzzleThe clues are in your language and the answers in the one you learn. You write the answers in my handwriting. A letter hint costs 10 coins and a word hint 30.
3 · Practise itSolved words go into three folders: Knew it, Knew with letters, Mostly revealed. You practise them there so you always remember them. Flash cards bring them back.
4 · Grow a gardenEach level from A1 to C1 has a garden with eight blooms. The words you learn plant them. Every milestone helps one of them bloom.
