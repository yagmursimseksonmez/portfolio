As Kitbox’s only designer, I designed its first shopping app for iOS end to end, working with the business owner, a PM and an iOS developer.

- **From website to app** The brand only sold through its website, then released its own app on the App Store. It later closed and the app was taken down.
- **3-step checkout** Kept the way from cart to order to three steps.
- **Design system** Colours, type and components the developer built from directly, and the brand could reuse.
