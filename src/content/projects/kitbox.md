As Kitbox’s only designer, I designed its furniture shopping app for iOS end to end, working 1:1 with an iOS developer.

- **3-step checkout** Redesigned the entire purchase funnel into a high-conversion, 3-step checkout to minimise drop-offs.
- **Design system** Created a design system with precise tokens and components for direct iOS development.
- **Reusable UI kit** Left the brand with a reusable UI kit to launch future features without design debt.
