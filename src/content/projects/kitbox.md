As Kitbox’s only designer, I designed its first shopping app for iOS end to end, working 1:1 with an iOS developer.

- **From website to app** The brand only sold through its website. Now it has its own app on the App Store.
- **3-step checkout** Kept the way from cart to order to three steps.
- **Design system** Colours, type and components the developer built from directly, and the brand can reuse.
